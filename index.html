Step 00 · before anything else

Set up your folders first.

Do this once and Claude always knows where your videos, references and finished edits go. One big folder, one small folder per video, and a few things every video shares.

📁
Video EditorYour one big folder. Open this folder in Claude's Code tab every time you edit.
Shared · every video uses these
📥 inbox/Not sure where something goes? Drop it here. Claude files it for you.
🎨 brand guidelines/The references you loved, saved as your permanent look.
🔊 brand guidelines/sounds/Your favourite sound effects, reused in every edit.
📝 my rules.mdAlways / never. "No whoosh sounds." "Captions never on my face."
📍 placements.mdWhere captions, cards and effects sit on screen.

One folder per video
📁 2026-11-13 morning routine/
🎥 raw/What you filmed. Never edited, never deleted.
💡 references/Reels you want this video to feel like.
✅ exports/Finished edits, ready to post.
📁 2026-11-20 next video/ … every new video gets its own, dated so they stay in order
Why not just raw / references / exports? Fine for one video. By video five you can't tell which take goes with which edit, and the reels you loved never turn into your style. Per-video folders keep each edit tidy. The shared folders make every edit look like you.
00
One time · 2 min

Make your folders

  • Make an empty folder called Video Editor (Desktop or Documents is fine)
  • In Claude, open the Code tab and pick that folder
  • Paste this prompt and say yes when it asks
Prompt 0 · folders
Set up this folder as my video editing workspace. Don't delete, move or overwrite anything that's already here.

1. Create these folders: "inbox", "brand guidelines" and "brand guidelines/sounds".
2. Create "my rules.md" with two headings, Always and Never, and one example line under each.
3. Create "placements.md" with headings for Captions, Cards and titles, Effects and Keep clear. Add one starter line under each, for example "Captions sit at chest height, never on my face".
4. Create "brand guidelines/README.md" where you'll list my favourite references and what I love about each.
5. Create a CLAUDE.md file with these house rules, so you follow them every time I open this folder:
   - Every new video gets its own folder named "YYYY-MM-DD short name" with raw, references and exports inside.
   - When I drop files in inbox, tell me which video you think each belongs to, then move it after I say yes. Footage I filmed goes to raw, reels or screenshots I like go to references, finished edits go to exports.
   - Save finished edits only in that video's exports folder. Never delete or overwrite anything in raw.
   - Before every edit, read my rules.md, placements.md and brand guidelines.
   - When I say a reference is a favourite, copy it into brand guidelines and add one line to its README on what I love about it.
   - When I give a note like "never do X" or "always put Y here", ask if I want it saved, then add it to my rules.md or placements.md.
6. Make my first video folder, with today's date and the name "my first video".
7. Show me the finished folder tree and explain each part in one plain line.

01
One time · 10–20 min

Set up the baseline

This installs the tools that let Claude actually edit video. The first time takes a while because the speech model downloads. Grab a coffee. You only do this once.

Prompt 1 · baseline
Install ffmpeg, Python 3.12, Pillow, numpy and a Whisper transcriber on this computer for me. Then run one short test transcription so the speech model downloads now. Explain every step in plain English and check each one works at the end.
ffmpegThe video engine. Cuts, joins, adds sound, exports.
Python 3.12The language Claude's editing scripts run on.
PillowDraws text and images: captions, cards, titles.
numpyFast maths on every frame: zooms, colours, moves.
WhisperTurns your voice into text with timings, so captions line up.

One time · 5 min

Install the free skill + 51 effects

This gives Claude my edit style starter and my whole free effects pack.

Prompt 2 · install
Download my edit skill from nate-style-edit-skill.zip and unzip it into ~/.claude/skills/ so the folder is ~/.claude/skills/nate-herk-style-edit. 03
Every video

Get your video ready

  • Phone vertical, face to camera, chest up
  • Leave space above your head and around your chest. That's where the effects go
  • Under 30 seconds is the sweet spot. Longer = more time and tokens
  • iPhone? Turn off Settings → Camera → Formats → HDR Video, or the colours come out flat

Optional · video too long? Only if you're using your own video (my demo clip is already trimmed). No script needed. Drag your video into Claude and paste this. Claude listens, writes the transcript and suggests the cuts. You just say yes or no.

Optional · trim it for me
My video is too long. Transcribe it word for word, then suggest which parts to cut so it lands under 35 seconds. Keep my hook and my voice. Cut repeats, retakes and filler first. Show me each cut as a list (time, the exact words, why), wait for my OK, then save the trimmed video next to the original.
04
Every video · the magic one

Edit my video

Drag your video into the Claude window, then paste this. It cuts the dead air, writes the cover title, builds the cards, captions every word, adds effects and free sound effects, and checks its own work.

Prompt 3 · edit
Edit my video with the nate-herk-style-edit skill. Follow every step in the skill, including the Mixkit sound effects step. Make the cover title and card titles short and punchy, in my words. Here are reels I love for the pace: [paste 2-3 reel links]. Before you hand it over, look at the sheet and both covers yourself and fix anything that looks off.

Not into text-heavy edits? That's okay. Save 2-3 reels you love into this video's references folder (download them or screen-record them), then paste this instead. Claude studies them and copies the style.

Alternative · edit like my references
I don't want a text-heavy edit. Edit my video in the style of the reels in this video's references folder instead.
1. Study each reference first. Pull a frame every half second and transcribe it, then write me a short style card: how often it cuts, zooms and punch-ins, captions (size, font style, colour, position, words at a time, or none), graphics and b-roll, colour and light, music and sound effects, and how the first 2 seconds hook.
2. Show me the style card and wait for my OK.
3. Edit my video to match it, using my own words and footage. Never put text over my face.
4. Before you hand it over, put 3 frames of my edit next to 3 frames of the reference and fix anything that doesn't match.
5. Save the style card in brand guidelines as [style name].md, so next time I can just say "edit like [style name]".

06
Level up

Build your storyboard

See your whole edit as cards, with two fresh ideas for every beat. Type a note under any card and Claude changes only that beat.

Prompt 4 · storyboard
Make me a storyboard of this edit as one page I can open.
One card per beat, in order. On each card show:
1. a still from that moment
2. the exact words I say
3. what is on screen now (caption, card or effect)
4. two other ideas for this beat, each in one plain line
Number every card. Put a notes box under each one where I can type "keep", "use idea 1" or my own change.
When I say "done, run my notes", change only the beats I wrote on, show me the new stills for just those beats, and wait for my OK before you render the video.

The first edit is really slow

Normal. The first run downloads the speech model (about 1.5 GB). After that, a 30-second video takes a couple of minutes.

The colours look flat or grey

That's iPhone HDR. Turn off HDR Video in Camera settings for your next video. For this one, paste: "My video looks washed out, it's probably HDR. Convert it to normal colours before editing."

I'm on Windows

Same prompts. If a step fails, paste: "I'm on Windows. Adapt every step of this to work on my computer and explain what you changed."

Captions or cards cover my face

Paste: "My face is cut off or covered at [second]. Fix the framing and re-render." Then next time, film with more space above your head.

It asks me for permission a lot

That's Claude being careful on your computer. Read what it wants to do, then say yes. It's only touching the folder you're working in.
