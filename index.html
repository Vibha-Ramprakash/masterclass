Step by step

From raw video to edited reel.

Copy each prompt, paste it into the Code tab in Claude, and say yes when it asks. Parts in yellow are yours to fill in.

01
One time · 10–20 min

Set up the baseline

This installs the tools that let Claude actually edit video. The first time takes a while because the speech model downloads. Grab a coffee. You only do this once.

Prompt 1 · baseline
Install ffmpeg, Python 3.12, Pillow, numpy and a Whisper transcriber on this computer for me. Then run one short test transcription so the speech model downloads now. Explain every step in plain English and check each one works at the end.
02
One time · 5 min

Install the free skill + 51 effects

This gives Claude my edit style starter and my whole free effects pack.

Prompt 2 · install
Download my edit skill from nate-style-edit-skill.zip and unzip it into ~/.claude/skills/ so the folder is ~/.claude/skills/nate-herk-style-edit. Then clone https://github.com/Vibha-Ramprakash/claude-video-effects into ~/.claude/skills/effects and run its setup script until it prints READY. Check both work, then tell me in plain English what you installed.
03
Every video

Get your video ready

  • Phone vertical, face to camera, chest up
  • Leave space above your head and around your chest. That's where the effects go
  • Under 30 seconds is the sweet spot. Longer = more time and tokens
  • iPhone? Turn off Settings → Camera → Formats → HDR Video, or the colours come out flat

Video too long? Paste your script here first:

Prompt 3 · trim the script
Cut this script to 35 seconds or less. Keep my voice and my hook.

[paste your script]
04
Every video · the magic one

Edit my video

Drag your video into the Claude window, then paste this. It cuts the dead air, writes the cover title, builds the cards, captions every word, adds effects and free sound effects, and checks its own work.

Prompt 4 · edit
Edit my video with the nate-herk-style-edit skill. Follow every step in the skill, including the Mixkit sound effects step. Make the cover title and card titles short and punchy, in my words. Here are reels I love for the pace: [paste 2-3 reel links]. Before you hand it over, look at the sheet and both covers yourself and fix anything that looks off.

06
Level up

Build your storyboard

See your whole edit as cards, so you can point at what to change instead of describing it.

Prompt 5 · storyboard
Make me a simple storyboard of this edit as a page I can open. One card per beat with a still, the words I say and the effect or card on screen, so I can tell you what to change. Then wait for my notes before you re-render.

Claude says a tool is missing or "not found"

Paste: "Something is missing. Check that ffmpeg, Python and Whisper are all installed and working, fix whatever isn't, then try my edit again."

The first edit is really slow

Normal. The first run downloads the speech model (about 1.5 GB). After that, a 30-second video takes a couple of minutes.

The colours look flat or grey

That's iPhone HDR. Turn off HDR Video in Camera settings for your next video. For this one, paste: "My video looks washed out, it's probably HDR. Convert it to normal colours before editing."

I'm on Windows

Same prompts. If a step fails, paste: "I'm on Windows. Adapt every step of this to work on my computer and explain what you changed."

Captions or cards cover my face

Paste: "My face is cut off or covered at [second]. Fix the framing and re-render." Then next time, film with more space above your head.

It asks me for permission a lot

That's Claude being careful on your computer. Read what it wants to do, then say yes. It's only touching the folder you're working in.
